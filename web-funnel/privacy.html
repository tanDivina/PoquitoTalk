Privacy Policy for PoquitoTalk

Last updated: August 2, 2026

PoquitoTalk ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website poquitotalk.hero-apps.com and the PoquitoTalk mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user data and voice translation processing.

1. Data Collection & Processing

PoquitoTalk processes text and voice inputs solely for generating real-time Spanish translations. Voice inputs are converted to text and translated via secure API endpoints (Google Gemini / Cloud Speech API). We do not sell or store personal audio recordings.

2. Payments & Subscriptions

All web payments are processed securely via Stripe and synchronized with RevenueCat Funnels. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support desk at support@hero-apps.com.
