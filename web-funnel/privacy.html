Privacy Policy & Data Security

Last updated: August 3, 2026 • Effective for PoquitoTalk Web & Mobile Apps

1. Overview & Commitment

PoquitoTalk ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website poquitotalk.hero-apps.com and the PoquitoTalk mobile application. We are committed to protecting user privacy, ensuring transparent data collection, and maintaining GDPR & CCPA compliance for expats, nomads, and local service providers in Panamá.

2. Speech & Text Translation Processing

PoquitoTalk processes text dictations and audio voice clips solely to generate real-time Panamanian Spanish translations:

  • No Permanent Audio Storage: Voice clips processed via Google Speech-to-Text and Gemma AI are used strictly in-memory during translation and are never stored on our permanent servers.
  • Local Audio Caching: Generated `.mp3` audio files for WhatsApp attachments are saved locally on your device's sandbox cache and can be cleared anytime.

3. Payments & Stripe Security

All subscription transactions (Credit Packs & Annual Passes) are processed securely via Stripe Checkout and synchronized with RevenueCat Purchases v9:

  • We never receive, store, or log raw credit card or banking details on our servers.
  • Purchases are linked strictly to your anonymous user ID or email address for subscription entitlements.

4. Cookies & Local Analytics

We use minimal, essential cookies and local storage (`localStorage` / `AsyncStorage`) to:

  • Maintain active subscription entitlement state.
  • Save user preferences (e.g. selected Voice Persona, bookmarked service phrases).
  • Store feedback submission history so you can track your sent requests.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full control over your data. You may at any time request data deletion, export your saved translations, or reset your intake onboarding state directly within the app's Settings screen.

For any privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or support questions, please contact our support desk:
