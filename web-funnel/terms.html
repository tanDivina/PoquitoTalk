2. Description of Service

PoquitoTalk is an assistive communication utility designed to help English-speaking expats, visitors, and residents communicate effectively in Panamanian Spanish with local contractors, businesses, and services in Bocas del Toro and across Panamá. Core features include speech-to-text translation, natural Spanish voice note generation for WhatsApp, phrase templates, an offline island dictionary, and a community contractor directory.
