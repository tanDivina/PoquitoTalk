### Core Aesthetic Components

1. **The Signature "Button / Googly" Eyes:**
   - **Structure:** Large, prominent white circles with thick soft-charcoal outlines and tiny pin-dot pupils.
   - **Innocent / Zen State:** Closed happy crescent arcs (`^ _ ^`) or wide eyes gently looking up with a soft smile.
   - **Bewildered / Panic State:** Perfectly round wide-open circles with microscopic off-center pupils, tiny stress/confusion mark, and an oversized floating question mark.

2. **Deadpan Sidekick Characters (The Comedy Secret):**
   - **The Deadpan Cloud:** Floating pale-blue cloud with a flat horizontal line mouth (`._.`) and unbothered side-eye pupils.
   - **Minimalist Flowers:** Simple flat 5-petal daisies with dot eyes and subtle smiles grounded at the base.

3. **Physical Medium Simulation:**
   - **Wax-Crayon Cross-Hatching:** Visible diagonal stroke textures on wings, tree branches, clothing, boots, and speech bubbles.
   - **Matte Opaque Gouache:** Rich, flat paint fills with slight organic edge bleed on heavy watercolor paper tooth.
   - **Soft Charcoal Outlines:** Never use harsh `#000000` pitch black; use deep charcoal (`#1F2937`) or dark forest green (`#064E3B`) with hand-drawn, slightly uneven line weights.

4. **Organic Botanical Framing:**
   - Undulating, wavy green vines running along the left and right vertical edges of the canvas with stylized teardrop leaves.

5. **Curated Color Palette:**
   - **Primary Subject (Parrot):** Lime / Apple Green (`#84CC16`, `#65A30D`).
   - **Secondary Accent (Wing):** Sky Cyan Blue (`#38BDF8`, `#0284C7`) with navy pencil hatches.
   - **Warm Signal (Beak):** Marigold Amber (`#F59E0B`, `#D97706`).
   - **Pop Details (Boots, Question Mark):** Poppy Red (`#EF4444`).
   - **Backdrop:** Sunny Buttercup / Pale Lime Linen (`#FEF9C3`, `#FDE047`, `#FBF9F5`).
