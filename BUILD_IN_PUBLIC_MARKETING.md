# 📣 PoquitoTalk — Build-in-Public & Marketing Documentation

## 1. Executive Summary
**PoquitoTalk** is being built in public by **Dorien Van den Abbeele** ([@DorienVibecodes](https://x.com/DorienVibecodes)) for the **RevenueCat Shipaton 2026** hackathon. The product is validated directly within the expat and traveler community of **Bocas del Toro, Panama**.

---

## 2. Validation Journey & User Feedback Milestones

### Initial Validation Wave (Bocas Expat Community Groups)
- **Community Interest**: 7 explicit local expats raised their hands stating *"This would be super useful for me!"* plus widespread likes and positive comments across local Facebook groups and WhatsApp chats.
- **Key Insight**: Expats in Bocas del Toro struggle daily when contacting local service providers (A/C repair, boat captains/water taxis, Starlink installers, plumbers, medical clinics) because local providers communicate almost exclusively via **WhatsApp Spanish voice notes**.
- **Iteration Focus**: Moving from general interest to deep 1-on-1 interviews and VIP Beta testing to refine user experience.

## 4. 1-on-1 Interview Framework (The 3 Golden Questions)

When conducting 10-minute coffee chats or WhatsApp voice exchanges with early testers:

1. **"What was the last local service in Bocas you tried contacting on WhatsApp, and what was frustrating about it?"**
   - *Purpose*: Identifies high-friction service categories and validates directory entries.
2. **"When a local technician sends you a Spanish voice note back, what do you currently do?"**
   - *Purpose*: Validates the 2-way conversation loop (Auto-Clipboard Spanish Reply Banner & Import Audio File features).
3. **"What is the 1 phrase you find yourself trying to translate over and over again?"**
   - *Purpose*: Direct source for new pre-translated service presets in `src/services/presets.ts`.

## 5. Growth Loops & Monetization Model

### B2C Subscription Model (RevenueCat)
- **Free Tier**: 10 translations / day + basic audio playback.
- **Pro Tier**: $4.99 / month or $19.99 / year for unlimited WhatsApp voice notes, all 4 studio voice personas (Diego, Mateo, Sofia, Valeria), and full Bocas service directory access.

### B2B Hotel & Expat Partner Program (`poquitotalk.hero-apps.com`)
- **Concept**: Boutique hotel & hostel owners in Bocas del Toro buy credit bundles ($4.99 / 100 credits or $19.99 annual passes) via Stripe Checkout on `poquitotalk.hero-apps.com` to issue to incoming guests.
- **Incentive**: Hotel owners receive free Pro access and commission rewards for every guest who upgrades to a paid pass.
- **Hackathon Alignment**: Qualifies directly for the **Funnel Vision Award — Stripe** ($15,000–$100,000 prize category for revenue funnels & growth loops).
