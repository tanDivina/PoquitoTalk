## 📌 Creative Summary for Video Editor / Creator

This video tells the story of **Shipathon Day 6** for **PoquitoTalk**. The key hook is an **"Aha!" moment**: Dorien thought she was just building a 1-way translation app for expats in Bocas del Toro, Panama, until a Reddit thread with local Panamanian contractors revealed that contractors struggled with language friction too. 

The breakthrough: We re-architected PoquitoTalk into a **2-Way Magic Web Walkie-Talkie** inside WhatsApp with zero app installation required for local service providers!
