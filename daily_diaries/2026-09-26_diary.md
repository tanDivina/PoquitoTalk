## 🚀 Part 1: What Shipped in PoquitoTalk v1.5.5

Version 1.5.5 is our most refined release yet, specifically engineered around real-world Caribbean usability, conversational fluency, and cognitive zero-burden:

### 1. The Post-WhatsApp Return Modal (`PostWhatsAppModal.tsx`)
In our earlier versions, when a user dispatched a Spanish voice note to a contractor via WhatsApp, they were dropped into the WhatsApp interface with no obvious prompt reminding them to return to PoquitoTalk when the contractor replied.

In v1.5.5, we introduced a brand-new guidance modal featuring **Poquito the Parrot**:
* Triggers cleanly upon return from WhatsApp dispatch.
* Explains the full 3-step loop:
  1. *Send the voice note in WhatsApp.*
  2. *When the contractor replies with audio, tap Share.*
  3. *Select PoquitoTalk to instantly decode and listen in English.*
* Includes 1-tap toggles to either dismiss or jump straight to the Voice Note Decoder.

### 2. High-Priority Android Return Notifications (`notificationService.ts`)
To make returning from external apps effortless on Android:
* Integrated a dedicated Android notification channel (`whatsapp-return`) with high priority (`AndroidImportance.MAX`).
* When a user taps to dispatch a message or opens a live walkie stream, a floating heads-up banner is scheduled to drop down over WhatsApp after 12 seconds:
  - *"🦜 PoquitoTalk • Return to Chat: Tap here to return to PoquitoTalk for automatic translation."*
  - Or for live channels: *"Capitán [Name] is on the link! Tap to return to your live stream."*
* If the user switches back to PoquitoTalk manually, the notification auto-cancels instantly to keep the notification tray clean.

### 3. "Listen English" Audio Playback in Conversation Threads (`ThreadViewModal.tsx`)
* When an incoming voice note from a contractor is translated into English text, users can now tap the new **"Listen English"** audio button to hear the translation spoken aloud with natural, native inflection.
* Respects speaker identity: incoming contractor audio maintains contractor voice identity so listening feels natural and authentic.

### 4. Interactive Voice Preview Audio Sampling (`SettingsModal.tsx` & `OnboardingScreen.tsx`)
* Added interactive audio test buttons for **Diego** and **Sofia** voice personas.
* Users can preview the exact vocal rhythm and tone before selecting their preferred voice for outgoing Spanish notes.

### 5. Conversation & Walkie-Talkie Deduplication Fixes (`conversations.ts` & `walkieTalkie.ts`)
* Fixed an edge-case bug where switching between WhatsApp and live walkie sessions could generate duplicate message bubbles.
* Implemented strict message deduplication via memoized display filters (`displayMessages`), ensuring an airtight, clean conversation history.

## 🛠️ Part 2: Overcoming the Island Network Build Trap

### The Challenge
Building native Android bundles on an island comes with real infrastructure challenges. Earlier this week, our `./gradlew bundleRelease` task threw a network timeout error while downloading `kotlin-compiler-embeddable-2.1.20.jar` from Maven Central. Because Gradle dependencies are cached incrementally, a single 15-second packet drop can stall an entire 30-minute release task.

### The Diagnostic & Resolution
1. **Targeted Latency Audit**: Before launching the build, we ran a direct HTTP/2 probe against Maven Central (`https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/`), verifying a crisp 200 OK response with sub-200ms latency.
2. **Warm Daemon Compilation**: Running `./gradlew bundleRelease` with our optimized JVM heap (`-Xmx2048m -XX:MaxMetaspaceSize=512m`) compiled all 451 actionable tasks cleanly in 11 minutes and 5 seconds.
3. **Cryptographic Release Signing**: Verified the production keystore (`poquitotalk-release-key.keystore`) and alias (`poquitotalk`), generating the signed 64.46 MB production bundle.

## 📲 Part 3: How Existing Users Get Notified to Update

One of the most important aspects of mobile app distribution is ensuring existing users get the latest improvements without friction. Here is how the update propagation works for all currently installed users:

### 1. Google Play Automatic Update Queue
Because v1.5.5 was deployed to the **`production` track** with `status: completed` and an incremented `versionCode` (4 > 3):
* **Automatic Background Updates**: Users with "Auto-update apps" enabled on Wi-Fi will have their installed app automatically updated by Google Play Services within 24 to 48 hours.
* **Play Store Update Notifications**: For users who have manual updates or notifications enabled, Google Play delivers an **"Updates Available" notification** directly to their Android notification center.
* **Store Listing Update Button**: Opening the PoquitoTalk listing on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.heroapps.poquitotalk) immediately shows the green **"Update"** button instead of "Open".

### 2. Waitlist & Direct Subscriber Broadcast
With v1.5.5 live, our launch email engine (`scripts/notify_launch.py`) is primed to notify our direct community subscribers and waitlist members that the latest release is live with new audio playback and WhatsApp return guidance.
